Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Budget Helper is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): only the owner's dedicated budget inbox. It reads notification emails from an allowlist of senders (card alerts, payment notifications) and moves processed messages to Trash. It never sends email and never permanently deletes anything.
  • Google Sheets: only the owner's budget spreadsheet, to add and adjust budget rows.
  • Google sign-in (openid email profile): to check that a person is on the owner's allowlist.

How data is used and stored

Email content is used only to suggest budget rows for review. It is stored in the owner's own cloud account (Amazon Web Services) and kept for as long as the owner keeps it. To draft descriptions and categories, parts of an email are sent to an AI model hosted in that same account (Amazon Bedrock), which does not use them for training. Data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
